## The Flywheel

Four concurrent digital products at Federal Bank's Digital Centre of Excellence, connected through a shared Oracle CX AI intelligence layer. Each product feeds behavioral signals into the platform; the platform returns intelligence back — making every product smarter as the system grows.

| Product | What It Does | Key Metric |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| **Federal 24×7** | Consumer onboarding | 82% TAT reduction, 2M+ profiles |
| **Fed-E-Remit** | Cross-border remittance | $2.4M volume, 23% abandon ↓ |
| **Federal e-Rupee** | CBDC (0-to-1 greenfield) | May 2025 GA, <12 banks live |
| **FedOne** | Enterprise business banking | 14hr saved/LC, 99.9% SLA |

## Architecture

This portfolio is itself a product — designed with the same progressive-disclosure principles used in Federal 24×7's Generate & Freeze architecture:

- **30-second scan**: Hero metrics + flywheel visualization
- **3-minute read**: Expandable case study cards with structured narratives
- **15-minute deep dive**: Full case studies, ADRs, and methodology on Notion
